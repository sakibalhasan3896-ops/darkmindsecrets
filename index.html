1. Gaslighting: How Someone Makes You Question Your Own Sanity

Imagine being 100% certain that you locked the front door, but someone you trust repeatedly tells you that you didn't. Over time, you begin to doubt your own memory, judgments, and reality. In psychology, this is called 'Gaslighting'. Manipulators use this chilling technique to slowly erode your self-esteem, forcing you to rely entirely on their version of the truth. They twist facts so perfectly that you end up questioning your own mental health while they gain absolute control over you.

2. Love Bombing: The Dangerous Trap of Instant Affection

At the very beginning of a relationship, someone showers you with endless compliments, sudden gifts, and undivided attention, making you feel like the center of the universe. But as soon as you are hooked, they suddenly turn cold, distant, and punishing. This psychological trap is known as 'Love Bombing'. Manipulators flood your brain with a massive dopamine rush early on so you become intensely addicted to their validation. Once they know you belong to them, they withdraw their affection to make you beg for it.
